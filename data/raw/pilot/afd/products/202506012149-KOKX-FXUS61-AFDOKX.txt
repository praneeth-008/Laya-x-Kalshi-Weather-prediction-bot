&&

.NEAR TERM /THROUGH MONDAY/...
Low pressure continues to depart to the northeast leading to a 
weakening pressure gradient. As high pressure tries to build in from 
the south and west, a few more lingering surface troughs will swing 
through. Aloft, a trough remains over the area and can be seen quite 
well looking at just visible/GeoColor satellite imagery given smoke 
from Canadian wildfires in the upper levels.

A shortwave rotating through the base of the trough will move 
towards the area this evening. There is not a ton of moisture 
available, but based on radar, have changed the slight chance 
wording to isold as weak cells enter the cwa.

With the weakening pressure gradient, winds weaken tonight. There 
will likely be some clouds around given the passing shortwave, but 
confidence is low in the exact extent of cloud cover. This leads to 
a bit of an uncertain low temperature forecast. Went with a blend of 
MOS and NBM for now, but cloud cover trends will have to be 
monitored and adjustments to temperatures may be needed.

The aforementioned smoke is expected to remain aloft and not make it 
to the surface at this time. This may result in milky looking skies 
as times and lead to some filtered sunshine.

&&

.SHORT TERM /MONDAY NIGHT THROUGH TUESDAY/...
Troughing aloft continues to influence the area through Monday as 
the axis doesn't pass through until Monday evening. However, thinking 
that cloud cover will be lower than today leading to mostly sunny 
skies and no chances of rain. Highs looks to be right around normal 
or a degree or two lower. With the weak pressure gradient and water 
temps still in the upper 50s there will be enough of a water-land
temperature difference for a sea breeze circulation. This 
likely keeps areas along the immediate coast a few degrees 
cooler.

High pressure finally establishes itself over the area a bit more on 
Tuesday, as heights aloft rise with a building mid and upper level 
ridge. This will be the start of a warmer stretch of weather. Sunny 
and dry with highs in the mid 70s to low 80s on Tuesday.

&&

.LONG TERM /TUESDAY NIGHT THROUGH SUNDAY/...
An upr ridge over the region will produce warm wx thru most of the 
extended. A strong warming trend kicks in Wed, with Thu the hottest 
day attm per the latest progs. The latest modeling suggests a slowly 
approaching cold front Thu, washing out over the area on Fri 
allowing temps to recharge.  Shwrs and tstms possible late Thu with 
the front and falling heights, then chances again Fri with the 
residual boundary. SW flow aloft may limit extensive aftn and eve 
development with the best dynamics W of the cwa, but low confidence 
this far out. A stronger front with the main upr trof arrives on Sat 
with more chances for shwrs and tstms. Could be some robust 
development if the timing occurs during peak heating. NBM temps may 
be too low Fri and especially Sat ahead of the main front.
